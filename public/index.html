Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common voting questions.

Do I need ID to vote?

Yes!

In India, you usually need your Voter ID (EPIC card). You can also use Aadhaar, PAN, or Passport if your name is in the roll.

How to register?

Easy!

You can register online using our "Register Online" button or via the official Voter Helpline App/NVSP portal.

Where to vote?

Find Booth

Your polling booth is usually in a nearby school or community hall. Check your booth location on the ECI website.
